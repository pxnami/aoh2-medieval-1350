1 January 1350. The Medieval World.
A historical revision of the original 1354 scenario on Earth 14K, with 13,892 provinces. Corrected borders, capitals and dependencies; 333 English decision events.
The plague strains the European realms. England and France contest the crown, the Serbian Empire reaches into Thessaly, and the Yuan court faces mounting unrest.
Province borders are fixed by the map. Some inherited minor polities and flag assets remain approximate.
