# Sources and review scope

The underlying map and scenario data are from the installed game's original **1354 scenario** (`map/Earth14K/scenarios/1749545365340dkbbwins`). The original scenario is credited to the game, not presented as a map drawn from scratch for this mod. The revision changes its date, ownership, dependencies, capitals and events.

Reviewed historical premises:

- England's French possessions and Calais: National Archives, https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/french-lands-english-kings/
- English-French conflict and the truce: Royal Armouries, https://royalarmouries.org/objects-and-stories/stories/the-hundred-years-war-1337-1453
- Plague chronology: National Portrait Gallery, https://portrait.gov.au/aboutface/2018/08/the-black-death
- Charles IV's position before his imperial coronation in 1355: Staatliche Museen zu Berlin, https://ikmk.smb.museum/object?id=18238850&lang=en&view=vs
- Serbia under Stefan Dusan: Blago Fund, https://www.blagofund.org/serbian-medieval-history/rulers/stefan-dusan?format=html
- Scandinavian dynastic background: Historiska museet, https://historiska.se/en/explore-history/history-hub/when-sweden-became-sweden/
- Yuan chronology and growing unrest: Metropolitan Museum of Art, https://www.metmuseum.org/ja/essays/yuan-dynasty-1271-1368
- Goryeo chronology: National Museum of Korea, https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0201040200.do?schM=list&showHallId=759&showroomCode=DM0036
- Chobanid territory and dates: Encyclopaedia Iranica, https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/chobanids-chupanids-pers/
- Jalayirid Baghdad: Encyclopaedia Iranica, https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/baghdad/baghdad-iranian-connection-ii/
- Kartid Herat: Encyclopaedia Iranica, https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/herat-iii/
- Injuid Fars and Sarbadar Khorasan: Encyclopaedia Iranica, https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/iran-ii2-islamic-period-page-3/?generate_pdf=1
- Japanese Northern and Southern Courts: Metropolitan Museum of Art, https://www.metmuseum.org/ja/essays/kamakura-and-nanbokucho-periods-1185-1392
- Ashikaga court in Kyoto: Princeton University Art Museum, https://static.artmuseum.princeton.edu/asian-art/japan/timeline
- John II's 1350 accession and coronation: Bibliotheque nationale de France, https://classes.bnf.fr/fouquet/grand/f039.htm
- Alfonso XI's death and the later Trastamara conflict: Daniel Cantor-Echols, University of Chicago dissertation, https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/record/3458/files/CantorEchols_uchicago_0330D_15913.pdf
- Comparative atlas for the European setting: Gustav Droysen, Allgemeiner Historischer Handatlas (1886), digitized reference listed at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:C._1350_Europe.jpg

Province assignments are adaptations to fixed game polygons, not GIS boundaries extracted from these sources. The primary sources above do not establish every individual polygon, every minor state's autonomy or every Japanese clan's allegiance. Those remain simplified or inherited. No historical census is implied by the game's population values. Flags for custom definitions derive from installed game assets and are not claims of original period flags.

Dates of later successor states and subdivisions also informed the correction list; exact local boundaries remain approximate. `HistoricalChanges.json` is the authoritative record of what this package actually changes.
