# Events and exact decision effects

There are **333 event instances in 14K** and **151 in 4K**. Country-specific copies of the same plague event are counted separately; these totals do not mean 333 or 151 different stories. Events are non-repeatable, require their recipient to exist and run after their eligibility date when the game next checks events. Their expiration is 1 January two years after their starting year.

## 14K events

### The Black Death

**Recipient:** One instance for each eligible European country (322).
**Eligible from:** 1350-01-02. **Expires:** 1352-01-01.

The plague has reached the realm. Fields lie abandoned, markets fall silent, and the court receives desperate appeals from towns and villages. Decide how to protect the people and keep the realm supplied.

| Choice | Effects | AI weight |
|---|---|---|
| Close ports and restrict travel | -1200 gold, -8% population, -8% economy, -5 happiness | 45% |
| Keep trade routes open | -16% population, -3% economy, -10 happiness | 25% |
| Fund hospitals and poor relief | -1800 gold, -11% population, -5% economy, +5 happiness | 30% |

Plague population and economy effects target the recipient's recorded starting provinces that it still owns. Happiness effects use the game's country-wide happiness outcome. Gold effects apply to the recipient's treasury. AI weights select a choice; they are not the probability that the event will appear.
